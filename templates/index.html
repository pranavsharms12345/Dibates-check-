Medical guidance

Fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL is in the prediabetes range and ≥126 mg/dL is in the diabetes range when confirmed using appropriate testing. Blood pressure, BMI, age, pregnancies, and pedigree score do not diagnose diabetes on their own.
